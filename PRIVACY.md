# SlitherZoomer: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 22, 2026*

SlitherZoomer is a browser extension and userscript for slither.io. This policy explains what it does with data.

## Data we collect

**None.** SlitherZoomer does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.

## Data stored on your device

SlitherZoomer saves your settings (zoom, render resolution, hotkeys, language, menu options and the optional fixed nickname) in your browser's local storage on the slither.io page. This data stays on your computer, is never sent anywhere by SlitherZoomer, and is removed when you clear site data for slither.io or uninstall the extension.

The fixed nickname you type is only written into the nickname field of the game, the same way as if you typed it yourself.

## Third-party requests

To display its menu font, SlitherZoomer loads the "Baloo 2" font from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Like any web request, this lets Google see your IP address and browser details. See the [Google Fonts privacy FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy) for details.

SlitherZoomer does not communicate with the slither.io game server and does not change any data sent to it.

## Permissions

The extension only runs on slither.io pages. It does not request access to any other website, your tabs, your browsing history or other browser data.
